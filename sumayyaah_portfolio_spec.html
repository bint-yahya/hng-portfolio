TYPOGRAPHY STACK

  • Display/Headings: Playfair Display — loaded from Google Fonts. Use for h1–h3, pull quotes, and the hero tagline. Weight 700 for hero, 600 for sub-headings.
  • Body: Inter — for all paragraph text, descriptions, and UI labels. Weight 400 regular, 500 medium.
  • Mono/Labels: Space Grotesk — for eyebrow labels, tags, nav links, category chips, and all-caps headings. Letter-spacing 0.08–0.15em depending on size.

TYPE SCALE

  • h1 — 3.5rem / Playfair Display 700
  • h2 — 2.2rem / Playfair Display 600
  • h3 — 1.5rem / Playfair Display 600
  • h4 — 1rem / Playfair Display 600
  • body — 0.95rem / Inter 400
  • label — 0.7rem / Space Grotesk 500, uppercase, tracked
  • tag — 0.65rem / Space Grotesk 500

BACKGROUND TEXTURE

Apply a subtle SVG grain noise texture as a pseudo-element overlay across the entire body. Opacity 0.025–0.035. This removes the flat, digital feel and adds tactility. Use: background-image: url("data:image/svg+xml,...noise-filter...") or a repeating PNG grain tile at low opacity.

BRAND STRATEGY

The Boring Product Campaign

OatLabs · April 2026 · Stage 2, Marketing Fellowship

Turned oats — the world's most "boring" food product — into a compelling DTC brand with full positioning, funnel, ad system, and a $0 → $10K revenue roadmap.

THE BRIEF

Overview

Stage 2 of the HNG Marketing Fellowship challenged interns to select a low-interest, everyday product and build a complete marketing strategy to drive it from $0 to $10K revenue. I chose oats — not despite the fact that they're boring, but because of it.

THE CHALLENGE

What made this hard

Oats have a retention problem: people buy once, eat them plain, get bored, and never come back. The category is large ($9.5B globally) but the repeat purchase rate collapses because nobody teaches consumers how to make them interesting. The brief wasn't just "market oats" — it was "make oats a product people talk about."

10 · MOTION SPEC

Animations & Interactions

Every animation should feel intentional, not decorative. Use Framer Motion throughout. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

PAGE LOAD / HERO REVEAL

Staggered entry: eyebrow (0.2s delay) → h1 line 1 (0.4s) → h1 line 2 (0.6s) → sub-line (0.8s) → CTAs (1.0s). Each slides up 24px from below with opacity 0→1. Easing: easeOut. Duration per item: 0.5s.

SCROLL REVEAL

Every section's content animates in when it enters the viewport. Use Framer Motion's whileInView: opacity: 0→1, y: 20→0, duration 0.5s, once:true. Stagger child elements by 0.1s.

CARD HOVER

Project cards: whileHover: {y: -4, boxShadow: "0 16px 40px rgba(196,119,58,0.15)"}. Border transitions to rgba(196,119,58,0.5). Duration: 0.2s. Smooth ease.

NAV SCROLL BEHAVIOUR

On scroll > 60px: nav background animates to rgba(9,9,14,0.92) with blur. Use a scroll event listener + CSS transition or Framer Motion layout animation. Transition: 0.3s ease.

MOBILE MENU

Hamburger → X rotation: 0.3s. Full-screen overlay slides in from right or fades in at 0.25s. Nav links stagger in from right: 0.05s delay per item. Close on link click with reverse animation.

PAGE TRANSITIONS

Between routes: outgoing page fades to opacity 0 over 0.2s, incoming page fades in from opacity 0 over 0.3s with a 20px upward slide. Use Framer Motion AnimatePresence with mode="wait".

CURSOR (DESKTOP ONLY)

Custom cursor: a small 8px circle in --accent that follows the mouse with a ~60ms lag (spring physics). On hovering links and buttons, the cursor scales up to 28px and transitions to a ring (hollow circle) to indicate interactivity. Hide the default CSS cursor site-wide: cursor: none. Re-enable on mobile/touch. Implement with a global cursor component using useState for position + CSS transform.
